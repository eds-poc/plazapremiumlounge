# Partner Festival Content: authoring guide

The intro of a partner festival offer (source `/en-uk/rockinthunder`): a small maroon pill above
a large heading, the description and small print, then the campaign CTAs as rounded boxes.

## Table

| Partner Festival Content | |
| --- | --- |
| Festival Exclusive Offer<br>## Rockin' Thunder festival travelers, this one's for you!<br>We're excited to bring you… **Rockin' Thunder** attendees…<br>Relax, recharge, and travel in style…<br>*Offer valid for a limited time only. Terms and conditions apply.* | |
| Campaign Period | June 28, 2026 – July 12, 2026 (MDT) |
| Lounge Pass Validity | 12 Months from Purchase |

- **Content (first row, one cell):**
  - **Eyebrow:** the line before the heading, shown as a pill.
  - **Heading:** any level (heading 2 on the source).
  - **Description:** the paragraphs after the heading.
  - **Small print:** a paragraph that is all *italic* is shown smaller and grey.
- **CTAs (one row each):** the label | the value. The first CTA is light (Campaign Period); the
  others are maroon (e.g. Explore Offer or Lounge Pass Validity). To make a CTA clickable, link
  the value (or the label); the whole box becomes the link.

Every part is optional: leave out a line or a row and the rest still lays out.

## Layout

Mobile first: full width with the text 16px and the heading 27pt. From 768px the block sits 32px
in from the section sides, and from 1024px the text keeps the source's 654px column width. Put the
block in a section without a background (white, as the source campaign area), before Partner
Feature Cards.
