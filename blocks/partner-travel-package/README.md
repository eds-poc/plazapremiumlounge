# Partner Travel Package: authoring guide

Redemption steps for a partner offer (source `/en-uk/rockinthunder`, "How to Redeem Your
Festival Travel Package"): a white rounded panel with a header and numbered steps.

## Table

| Partner Travel Package | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEAMLESS CONVENIENCE<br>### How to Redeem Your Festival Travel Package | | |
| 1 | Purchase Online | Secure your special Americas Lounge Pass or Vino Volo Dining Deal… |
| 2 | Receive Instant Digital Voucher | Check your email inbox immediately for your digital pass… |
| 3 | Relax, Recharge, Dine | Scan your ticket at participating airport lounges… |

- **Header (first row, one cell):** the **Section Eyebrow** (the line before the heading) and the
  **Section Heading** (any level; heading 3 on the source).
- **Steps (one row each):** **Step Number** | **Step Title** | **Step Description**. Leave the
  number cell out (two cells: title | description) and the steps are numbered in order.

Add or delete step rows to change the number of steps. The step titles are one heading level
below the section heading.

## Layout

Mobile first: the steps one under the other, centred. From 768px three steps across, joined by a
dashed line between the number circles, and the panel 32px in from the section sides. The circles
are maroon, plum and the action maroon in turn, as on the source. Put the block in a section
without a background, after Partner Feature Cards.
